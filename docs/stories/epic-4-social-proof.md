# Epic 4: Social Proof & Authority
**Priority**: P1

## Overview
This epic focuses on building trust and demonstrating expertise through case studies and client results.

## User Stories

### Story 4.1: Case Studies with Real-world Metrics
- **As a** business owner
- **I want** to see case studies with specific performance metrics (e.g., "200% traffic increase")
- **So that** I can trust the ROI of WTF DEV's services.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Given a case study card, when viewed, then it must display at least one "Hero Metric" clearly (Large bold number).
- [ ] Given I click on a case study, then it expands to show a brief "Challenge - Solution - Result" summary.
- [ ] Edge case: No image for case study -> Use a branded tech-style placeholder image.
- [ ] Edge case: Metric is very small -> Don't show "0%" or negligible numbers; filter out low-impact studies.

**Story Points**: 3
